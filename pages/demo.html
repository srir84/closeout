Closeout: the demo

Closeout turns finished home repairs into verified records a homeowner can show when they sell. This walkthrough was recorded from the running demo. Run it yourself with make demo.

What happens

  1. A contractor uploads a finished job and its invoice. AI reads the invoice twice, and the two reads must agree.
  2. The contractor approves the exact emails. Nothing is sent before that.
  3. The homeowner confirms from the email link. The job gets three marks: homeowner, contractor and town permit.
  4. The homeowner asks the town about an old permit. The clerk's letter is read twice, and the result is shown only if both reads agree.
  5. The sale report, and a share link for a buyer's agent with prices hidden.
  6. Ops: the review list and privacy requests. Then the real public permit data.

Every person, home, company, invoice and letter in this demo is made up. Emails go only to a local test mailbox. The permit and parcel data on the data page is real public data from New Jersey, with no people in it. See the clickable mock · Source
